
1. Project Title
All in One – Online Shopping 

## 2. Introduction

All in One is an Android-based multi-vendor online shopping application designed to bring different product categories and vendors together in one platform.

Customers can explore and purchase different types of products, including clothing, food, groceries, electronics, gadgets, beauty products, shoes, and other daily necessities.

Instead of visiting different shopping websites, mobile applications, or physical stores, customers can access multiple vendors and product categories through a single application.

The platform connects customers, vendors, and delivery personnel to provide a convenient shopping and delivery experience.

## 3. Problem Statement

Customers currently face several difficulties when shopping for different products.

- They need to visit multiple websites and applications to purchase different types of products.
- Managing different accounts across shopping platforms is inconvenient.
- Customers must manage separate shopping carts and orders.
- Finding suitable products and vendors takes time.
- Visiting multiple physical shops requires additional time and effort.
- Managing different payment and delivery services can be complicated.
- Small and local vendors may have limited opportunities to reach customers online.

All in One aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized multi-vendor shopping platform.

## 4. Project Objectives

- Develop a user-friendly Android shopping application.
- Bring multiple product categories into one platform.
- Connect customers with multiple vendors.
- Provide product browsing and searching.
- Support shopping cart and checkout functionality.
- Provide convenient payment options.
- Integrate location services and Google Maps.
- Support order management.
- Improve shopping convenience.
- Reduce the need to use multiple shopping applications.

## 5. Target Users

### Customers
Browse products, search for items, add products to favourites and cart, and place orders.

### Vendors / Shop Owners
Manage shop products, receive orders, and approve or decline customer orders.

### Delivery Personnel
Access relevant order and customer location information to support deliveries.

## 6. Product Categories

- Clothing and Fashion
- Food and Groceries
- Electronics and Gadgets
- Beauty and Cosmetics
- Shoes and Footwear
- Other Daily Necessities

## 7. Technology Stack

- Java and Kotlin
- Android Studio
- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Database
- Firebase Storage
- Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Google Maps and Location Services
- Glide and Picasso
- Paystack

## 8. Project Vision

To create a unified online marketplace where customers can purchase different types of products from multiple vendors through one convenient application.

## 9. Expected Outcomes

- A functional Android application.
- Multiple shopping categories.
- Access to multiple vendors.
- Convenient product browsing and ordering.
- Shopping cart and checkout.
- Payment options.
- Location-based delivery support.
- Improved customer shopping experience.

## 10. Conclusion

All in One aims to simplify online shopping by combining different product categories, vendors, and delivery-related services into one centralized application.
