
# Risk Management

## 1. Introduction

Risk management identifies possible problems that could affect the development or operation of All in One.

## 2. Identified Risks

### Risk 1: Internet Problems
Impact: High

Solution:
- Handle network errors.
- Display connection messages.
- Test different network conditions.

### Risk 2: Firebase Failure
Impact: High

Solution:
- Check Firebase configuration.
- Handle database errors.
- Test Firebase operations.

### Risk 3: Incorrect Location
Impact: High

Solution:
- Validate location permissions.
- Check location coordinates.
- Allow customers to verify their location.

### Risk 4: Payment Failure
Impact: High

Solution:
- Handle failed transactions.
- Verify payment results.
- Display payment error messages.

### Risk 5: Application Crashes
Impact: High

Solution:
- Perform regular testing.
- Handle unexpected exceptions.
- Fix identified bugs.

### Risk 6: Incorrect Order Details
Impact: High

Solution:
- Validate product quantities.
- Verify order information.
- Check customer addresses.

### Risk 7: Notification Failure
Impact: Medium

Solution:
- Check Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- Test notification delivery.

### Risk 8: Team Communication Problems
Impact: Medium

Solution:
- Conduct regular meetings.
- Assign clear tasks.
- Share progress updates.

## 3. Risk Monitoring

The team will regularly review risks and take appropriate action when problems occur.

## 4. Conclusion

Risk management helps improve application reliability and reduce project development problems.
