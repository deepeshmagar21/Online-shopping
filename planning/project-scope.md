
# Project Scope

## 1. Introduction

The scope of All in One defines the features and services provided by the multi-vendor shopping application.

## 2. Main Features

### User Management
- Registration
- Login
- Forgot password
- User profile management

### Multi-Category Shopping
- Clothing and fashion
- Food and groceries
- Electronics and gadgets
- Beauty products
- Shoes and footwear
- Other products

### Product Management
- Product browsing
- Product details
- Product categories
- Product search
- Product favourites

### Shopping Management
- Shopping cart
- Add and remove products
- Quantity management
- Checkout
- Order placement

### Payment
- Pay on Delivery
- Debit and credit card options
- Paystack integration

### Location and Delivery
- Google Maps integration
- Customer location selection
- Customer location viewing
- Delivery-related order management

### Notifications
- Order notifications
- Order status notifications

## 3. Out-of-Scope Features

Possible future improvements include:

- Advanced delivery route optimization.
- AI-based product recommendations.
- Advanced vendor analytics.
- Loyalty and rewards programs.
- Additional payment gateways.
- Advanced promotional campaigns.

## 4. Project Limitations

- Internet connectivity is required for online services.
- Location features require device permissions.
- Google Maps requires valid API configuration.
- Payment services depend on gateway configuration.

## 5. Target Platform

Android mobile application.

## 6. Conclusion

All in One provides a centralized shopping platform for customers to explore different products and vendors and manage their shopping activities through one application.
