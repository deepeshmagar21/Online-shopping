# RACI Matrix

## 1. Introduction

The RACI Matrix defines the roles and responsibilities of the three team members working on the All in One – Online Shopping and Multi-Vendor Delivery Ecosystem project.

RACI stands for:

- **R – Responsible:** The person who performs the task.
- **A – Accountable:** The person who takes ownership of the task.
- **C – Consulted:** The person who provides advice or feedback.
- **I – Informed:** The person who receives updates about the task.
